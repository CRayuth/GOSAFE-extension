GOSAFE adblock Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

What the extension does

GOSAFE adblock blocks ads, trackers, and some phishing/malware patterns in your browser. It also offers optional helpers (YouTube/Spotify, cosmetics, HTTPS upgrade, User-Agent spoofing, and similar protections).

Data collection

We do not collect, sell, or transmit your browsing history to our servers. Filtering and settings run locally in your browser.

  • Settings, site rules, custom hide selectors, activity log, and User-Agent preferences are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device.
  • Optional filter-list auto-update may download public blocklists from third-party mirrors you already rely on for ad blocking. Those requests do not include a personal account.
  • The extension needs broad site access (<all_urls>) to apply network and page protections wherever you browse.

Permissions

Permissions such as storage, tabs, declarativeNetRequest, downloads, privacy, and alarms are used only to implement the features described in the store listing (blocking, optional download/WebRTC/UA controls, and local activity stats).

Filter lists

Network and cosmetic rules are built from public community lists (including EasyList, EasyPrivacy, and lists published by the uBlock Origin uAssets project). Those lists are third-party data; this extension is not affiliated with uBlock Origin.
